# MM01

**Area:** Mental Models (MM) · **Topic:** How GenAI works

> *At the end of the course, students should be able to* …
>
> **explain how GenAI models generate output using a simplified “next-word prediction” process, recognizing that real systems predict tokens and use contextual information from prompts and prior text to determine their responses.**

## Why it is included

Builds the core mental model, dispelling the retrieval and reasoning myths behind most misuse; “next-word” stands in for token prediction to suit first-years (CS02 adds the detail).
