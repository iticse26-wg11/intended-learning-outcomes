# EPR01

**Area:** Ethics, Policy and Regulations (EPR) · **Sub-area:** Resources and labor for GenAI · **Topic:** Resources and Labor

> *At the end of the course, students should be able to* …
>
> **understand the origins of resources (e.g., water, minerals, energy, human labor) required to train, deploy, and host large-scale AI models.**

## Why it is included

Builds sustainability awareness by tracing what large-scale AI consumes: water, minerals and energy, and human labor from mining to data labelling and to creators whose work enters training data, often unknowingly (EPR02).
