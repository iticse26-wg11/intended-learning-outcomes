# EPR12

**Area:** Ethics, Policy and Regulations (EPR) · **Sub-area:** GenAI in Social, Professional, and Regulatory Contexts · **Topic:** AI Policy

> *At the end of the course, students should be able to* …
>
> **identify and interpret relevant GenAI policies, assessing their applicability to and impacts on GenAI use in different contexts.**

## Why it is included

Supports policy-aware judgement: students locate the policies that apply to them (academic integrity, data protection, workplace AI-use rules), read them, and work out what they permit or forbid in a given context (EPR04).
