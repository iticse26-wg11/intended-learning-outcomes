# CS03

**Area:** Computer Science (CS) · **Sub-area:** Foundations of GenAI · **Topic:** Open source AI models vs proprietary models

> *At the end of the course, students should be able to* …

> **compare open-source and proprietary GenAI models and identify their main advantages and disadvantages for a given context, for example in terms of data privacy.**

## Why it is included

Makes licensing and deployment a conscious choice: open-source, open-weight or proprietary, and local, institution-hosted or third-party API, decide who sees the data and who controls the model, plus cost and performance.
