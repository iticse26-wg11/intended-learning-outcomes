# Ethics, Policy and Regulations (EPR)

All outcomes complete the stem: **"At the end of the course, students should be able to …"**

## Resources and labor for GenAI

| ID | Topic | ILO |
|----|-------|-----|
| **EPR01** | Resources and Labor | … understand the origins of resources (e.g. water, minerals, human labor) required to train, deploy, and host large-scale AI models |

## GenAI and Data

| ID | Topic | ILO |
|----|-------|-----|
| **EPR02** | Input Data, Bias | … identify data sources used to train GenAI models and describe how training data quality, consent, and representation shape GenAI model behaviors. |
| **EPR03** | Data Control | … evaluate data management in GenAI systems in order to identify risks of misuse, manipulation, and threats to data sovereignty. |
| **EPR04** | Output Data | … explain the shortcomings of GenAI outputs (e.g. accuracy, reliability) and recognise the need to follow the appropriate policies and regulations within the context where the outputs will be used. |

## Limitations, Efficacy and Alignment

| ID | Topic | ILO |
|----|-------|-----|
| **EPR05** | Model Limitations | … recognize limitations in GenAI model outputs, including hallucination, misinformation, privacy leakage, harmful content, in the context of societal impacts. |
| **EPR06** | Measures of Efficacy | … differentiate between reliable, trustworthy, and responsible GenAI systems. |
| **EPR07** | Alignment Practices | … identify tools and procedures for evaluating and verifying model outputs, and apply these to assess AI-generated content. |

## GenAI Use and Responsibility

| ID | Topic | ILO |
|----|-------|-----|
| **EPR08** | Cognitive Debt | … explain how overreliance on GenAI can lead to cognitive debt. |
| **EPR09** | Labor and Automation | … analyze the effects of GenAI automation on human labor, workplace expectations, and the labor market. |
| **EPR10** | Evaluating AI Use | … evaluate (the use of) GenAI systems against relevant normative principles (e.g., fairness, transparency, accountability, safety, robustness, and human governance). |
| **EPR11** | Responsible Use | … design a plan for the responsible and context-appropriate use of GenAI, including documenting of GenAI-related outputs and decisions |

## GenAI in Social, Professional, and Regulatory Contexts

| ID | Topic | ILO |
|----|-------|-----|
| **EPR12** | AI Policy | … identify and interpret relevant GenAI policies, assessing their applicability to and impacts on GenAI use in different contexts. |
| **EPR13** | Societal Influences, Expectations | … discuss societal perceptions of and expectations around GenAI use, taking into consideration power dynamics in the adoption of GenAI tools. |
| **EPR14** | Discipline-Specific AI Norms | … discuss frameworks for the fair and responsible use of GenAI in the student's particular discipline. |
